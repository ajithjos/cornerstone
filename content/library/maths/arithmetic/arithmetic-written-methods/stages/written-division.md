# Written Division

This stage teaches short division and long division for whole numbers.

## Plain-Language Meaning

The learner divides one place at a time, records the quotient in the correct place, and checks the result with multiplication.

## Skills In This Stage

- `interpret_division_as_sharing_and_grouping`
- `divide_whole_numbers_with_written_methods`
- `check_written_division_with_multiplication`

## Secure Performance

The learner can divide whole numbers by one-digit and multi-digit divisors, handle remainders correctly, and prove the answer with `divisor x quotient + remainder`.
