# Compare And Order Whole Numbers

- successful performance: compares and orders whole numbers by checking the largest place first, then moving right
- likely mistakes: compares the final digit first or assumes the number with the largest-looking digit is always greater
- out of scope: negative numbers, decimals, and fractions
