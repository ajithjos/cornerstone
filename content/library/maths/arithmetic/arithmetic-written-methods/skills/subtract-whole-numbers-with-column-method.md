# Subtract Whole Numbers With Column Method

- successful performance: aligns whole numbers by place value, subtracts from the ones column leftward, exchanges from the next available place when needed, and records the answer accurately
- likely mistakes: subtracts the smaller digit from the larger regardless of place, exchanges from the wrong column, or mishandles zeros in the top number
- out of scope: negative answers, decimal subtraction, and fraction subtraction
