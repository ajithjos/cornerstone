# Add Whole Numbers With Column Method

- successful performance: aligns whole numbers by place value, adds from the ones column leftward, exchanges when a column total is `10` or more, and records the answer accurately
- likely mistakes: misaligns columns, forgets an exchange, writes the whole column total in one place, or treats the carried digit as a loose extra number
- out of scope: decimal addition, fraction addition, and algebraic notation
