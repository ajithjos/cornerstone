# Interpret Division As Sharing And Grouping

- successful performance: explains division as sharing into equal parts or grouping into equal sets and can tell what a quotient and remainder mean
- likely mistakes: mixes up the number of groups with the size of each group, ignores the remainder, or records a remainder larger than the divisor
- out of scope: fraction quotients and decimal division answers
