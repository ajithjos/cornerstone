# Partition Whole Numbers By Place Value

- successful performance: partitions whole numbers into place-value parts and recombines those parts accurately
- likely mistakes: treats digits as separate counts without place-value meaning or drops zero placeholders
- out of scope: decimal partitioning and algebraic notation
