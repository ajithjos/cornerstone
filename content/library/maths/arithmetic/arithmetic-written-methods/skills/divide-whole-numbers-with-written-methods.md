# Divide Whole Numbers With Written Methods

- successful performance: divides whole numbers using short division for one-digit divisors and long division for multi-digit divisors, recording each quotient digit in the correct place
- likely mistakes: brings down digits without subtracting first, guesses quotient digits without checking, or leaves a remainder greater than or equal to the divisor
- out of scope: decimal quotients, fraction notation, and division by zero
