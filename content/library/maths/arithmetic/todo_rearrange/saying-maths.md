# Quick Guide: How to Say Math Symbols in Native English

A simple reference guide for reading math symbols aloud in everyday conversation and professional settings.

---

## 1. The Four Basic Operations

| Operation | Symbol | Example | Everyday / Native Way | Professional / Academic Way |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Addition** | `+` | 3 + 7 | "3 **plus** 7" | "The **sum of** 3 and 7" <br> "3 **added to** 7" |
| **Subtraction** | `-` | 7 - 3 | "7 **minus** 3" | "7 **subtract** 3" <br> "The **difference between** 7 and 3" |
| **Multiplication** | `x` or `*` | 3 x 7 | "3 **times** 7" | "3 **multiplied by** 7" |
| **Division** | `/` or `÷` | 7 / 3 | "7 **divided by** 3" | "7 **over** 3" (Advanced math) <br> "7 **out of** 3" (Fractions/Ratios) |

---

## 2. Text Symbols & Formatting

| Symbol | Example | Usage Context | Everyday / Native Way | Professional Way |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Underscore** | `_` | 3_7 | Coding / File names | "3 **underscore** 7" | "3 **under-bar** 7" |
| **Hyphen / Dash** | `-` | 3-7 | **A Range** (Ages, Dates) | "3 **to** 7" | "3 **through** 7" (American style) |

---

## 3. The Equals Sign (`=`)

When completing the math equation (e.g., `3 + 7 = 10`), you can say:

* **Everyday:** "3 plus 7 **is** 10" or "3 plus 7 **equals** 10"
* **Professional:** "3 plus 7 **is equal to** 10" or "3 plus 7 **makes** 10"

---

## 💡 Keyboard Shortcuts for Division (`÷`)
If you ever need to type the actual division sign instead of a slash `/`:
* **Windows:** Hold `Alt` and type `0247` on the number pad.
* **Mac:** Press `Option` + `/`.
