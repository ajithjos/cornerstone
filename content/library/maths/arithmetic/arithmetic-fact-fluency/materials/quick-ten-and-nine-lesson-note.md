# Quick ways with 10 and 9

**Add 10.** Jump forward one ten. **37 + 10 = 47.**

**Subtract 10.** Jump back one ten. **37 − 10 = 27.** The ones digit stays the same, even across a hundred: **103 − 10 = 93**.

**Add 9.** Add ten. That is one too many, so take one off.

**37 + 9 = 37 + 10 − 1 = 46**

**Subtract 9.** Take away ten. You took one too many, so give one back.

**37 − 9 = 37 − 10 + 1 = 28**

Try **54 + 9** and **54 − 9**. Say your jump of ten aloud. Check: **64 − 1 = 63** and **44 + 1 = 45**.
