# Bridge Through 10 Addition Practice

Use the make-`10` method. Say the split before you answer.

## Part A: Basic bridge-through-10 facts

```text
8 + 3 =
9 + 2 =
7 + 4 =
8 + 4 =
9 + 5 =
6 + 5 =
```

## Part B: Harder bridge-through-10 facts

```text
7 + 8 =
6 + 9 =
8 + 7 =
5 + 8 =
9 + 8 =
6 + 7 =
```

## Part C: Explain the method

```text
Show how 8 + 6 becomes 8 + __ + __.
Show how 7 + 9 becomes 7 + __ + __.
```

Coach check: if the learner answers correctly but cannot explain the split, the method is not secure yet.
