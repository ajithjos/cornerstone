# Division Facts From Tables Lesson Note

Division facts come from multiplication facts.

If you know:

```text
7 x 8 = 56
```

then you also know:

```text
56 / 7 = 8
56 / 8 = 7
```

## The fact family

For `6`, `9`, and `54`, the family is:

```text
6 x 9 = 54
9 x 6 = 54
54 / 6 = 9
54 / 9 = 6
```

Multiplication builds the total. Division starts with the total and finds the missing group size or number of groups.

## Check by multiplying

Example: `72 / 8`

Think:

```text
8 x ? = 72
8 x 9 = 72
72 / 8 = 9
```

Check:

```text
9 x 8 = 72
```

## Good learner self-talk

- `Which multiplication fact matches this division?`
- `What number times the divisor gives the dividend?`
- `Can I multiply back to check?`

## Quick spoken practice

1. Use `7 x 6 = 42` to answer `42 / 7`.
2. Use `8 x 9 = 72` to answer `72 / 9`.
3. Check `63 / 7 = 9` by multiplying.
