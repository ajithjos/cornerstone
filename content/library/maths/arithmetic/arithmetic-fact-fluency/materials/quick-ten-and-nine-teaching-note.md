# Teaching quick ways with 10 and 9

Start after the child can make ten and go back through ten. Start with 37 and add or remove one bundle of ten. Ask what stays the same. Then add nine by giving ten and taking one back. To subtract nine, take ten and give one back. Ask the child to explain the last move before practising.
