# Bridge Through 10 Subtraction Practice

Use the bridge-through-`10` method. Say the split before you answer.

## Part A: Basic bridge-through-10 facts

```text
11 - 3 =
12 - 4 =
13 - 5 =
14 - 6 =
15 - 7 =
16 - 8 =
```

## Part B: Harder bridge-through-10 facts

```text
15 - 8 =
17 - 9 =
18 - 9 =
16 - 7 =
14 - 8 =
19 - 8 =
```

## Part C: Explain the method

```text
Show how 13 - 6 becomes 13 - __ - __.
Show how 18 - 9 becomes 18 - __ - __.
```

Coach check: if the learner still counts backwards one by one, reteach the stopping point at `10`.
