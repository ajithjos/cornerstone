## Part B: Switched facts

Use commutativity. Say both facts before writing the missing answer.

| Known fact | Switched fact |
|---|---|
| 4 x 9 = 36 | 9 x 4 = ____ |

| 6 x 5 = 30 | 5 x 6 = ____ |
