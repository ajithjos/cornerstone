# Fact Families Through 10 Lesson Note

This is the reference page for addition families through `10`.

The main facts to memorise are the addition pairs for each whole from `1` to `10`.

Subtraction should come from those known addition facts. Do not treat subtraction as a separate random table.

## How to read a fact family

A fact family has:

- one whole
- two parts
- addition facts that build the whole
- subtraction facts that start with the whole and remove one part

Example:

```text
3 + 2 = 5
2 + 3 = 5
5 - 3 = 2
5 - 2 = 3
```

The whole is `5`. The parts are `3` and `2`.

## Read subtraction from addition

Use the addition family to answer subtraction.

Example:

```text
2 + 4 = 6
6 - 2 = 4
6 - 4 = 2
```

For whole `5`, the subtraction facts are:

```text
5 - 0 = 5
5 - 1 = 4
5 - 2 = 3
5 - 3 = 2
5 - 4 = 1
5 - 5 = 0
```

## Worked example: `6 - 2`

Ask:

```text
What goes with 2 to make 6?
```

Use the known addition fact:

```text
2 + 4 = 6
```

So:

```text
6 - 2 = 4
```
