# Fact Families Through 10 Lesson Note

This page teaches every whole from `1` to `10` as a family of facts.

## How to use this page

Say each total aloud. Then say the matching subtraction facts from the same family.

## Read subtraction from the same family

If `3 + 2 = 5`, then:

```text
5 - 3 = 2
5 - 2 = 3
```

If `1 + 0 = 1`, then:

```text
1 - 1 = 0
1 - 0 = 1
```

## Worked examples

### Example 1: `4 + 3`

```text
4 + 3 = 7
7 - 4 = 3
7 - 3 = 4
```

### Example 2: `6 - 2`

```text
Ask: what goes with 2 to make 6?
2 + 4 = 6
6 - 2 = 4
```

## Say this to yourself

```text
I know the whole and the two parts.
Subtraction is the same family backwards.
Zero is still a real part of the fact family.
```
