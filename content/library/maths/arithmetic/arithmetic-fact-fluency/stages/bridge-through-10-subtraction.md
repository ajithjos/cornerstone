# Bridge Through 10 Subtraction

This stage teaches how to derive subtraction facts through `20` by stepping back to `10` first.

## Plain-Language Meaning

The learner can split the number being subtracted into the part that gets back to `10` and the part still left to subtract.

## Skills In This Stage

- `bridge_through_10_for_subtraction`

## Secure Performance

The learner answers subtraction facts through `20` by stepping back to `10` first instead of counting back one by one.
