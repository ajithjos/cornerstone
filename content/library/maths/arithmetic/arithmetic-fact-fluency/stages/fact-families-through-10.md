# Fact Families Through 10

This stage teaches totals `1` to `10` in order and reads subtraction from the same families.

## Plain-Language Meaning

The learner no longer treats basic sums and take-away facts as separate random questions.

## Skills In This Stage

- `compose_numbers_to_10`
- `subtract_within_10_from_known_facts`

## Secure Performance

The learner can give the addition pairs for totals through `10`, including zero facts, and can answer the matching subtraction facts without restarting from `1`.
