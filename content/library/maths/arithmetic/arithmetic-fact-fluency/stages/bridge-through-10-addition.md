# Bridge Through 10 Addition

This stage teaches how to derive addition facts through `20` by making `10` first.

## Plain-Language Meaning

The learner can split one addend so the first step lands on `10`, then add the leftover part.

## Skills In This Stage

- `bridge_through_10_for_addition`

## Secure Performance

The learner answers addition facts through `20` by bridging through `10` and can explain the split aloud.
