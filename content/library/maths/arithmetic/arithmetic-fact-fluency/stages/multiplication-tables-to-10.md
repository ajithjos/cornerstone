# Multiplication Tables Through 10

This stage teaches multiplication facts through `10 x 10` as facts to know and use.

## Plain-Language Meaning

The learner sees multiplication as equal groups or arrays, then memorises the table facts until the facts are quick in mixed order.

## Skills In This Stage

- `recall_multiplication_facts_to_10`
- `use_commutativity_for_multiplication`

## Secure Performance

The learner answers multiplication facts through `10 x 10` accurately, recognises `6 x 8` and `8 x 6` as the same total, and can explain a fact with equal groups or an array when asked.
