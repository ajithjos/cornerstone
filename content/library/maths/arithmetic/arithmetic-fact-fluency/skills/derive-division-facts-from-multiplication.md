# Derive Division Facts From Multiplication

- successful performance: uses a known multiplication fact to answer the matching exact division facts
- likely mistakes: guesses a quotient without naming the related multiplication fact or mixes up divisor and quotient
- out of scope: division with remainders, decimal quotients, and long division
