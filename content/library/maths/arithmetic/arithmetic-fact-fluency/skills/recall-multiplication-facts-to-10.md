# Recall Multiplication Facts To 10

- successful performance: answers multiplication facts from `0 x 0` through `10 x 10` accurately in mixed order after direct teaching and practice
- likely mistakes: relies on slow skip-counting for every fact, omits zero and one facts, or confuses nearby table facts such as `7 x 8` and `6 x 8`
- out of scope: written multiplication with multi-digit partial products
