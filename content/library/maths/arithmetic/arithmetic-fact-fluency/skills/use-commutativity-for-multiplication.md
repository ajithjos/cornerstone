# Use Commutativity For Multiplication

- successful performance: recognises that switching the factors keeps the product the same, such as `4 x 7 = 7 x 4`
- likely mistakes: relearns both orders as unrelated facts or changes the answer when the factors switch
- out of scope: formal algebraic properties or proof language
